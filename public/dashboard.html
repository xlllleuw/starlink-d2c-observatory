ABOUT THIS OBSERVATORY

Bring the research onto the globe.

Satellite positions are propagated from public TLE data with SGP4 for the current UTC time. The display includes the ephemeris source, epoch and age. Positions update every second; orbital data is cached according to the source's update cycle.

The TAC layer uses the research's Fibonacci / spherical Voronoi candidate model, spanning approximately 70° north to 70° south. Selecting a country shows every TAC cell intersecting its boundaries, with border cells kept intact. A TAC and PLMN together identify a tracking area; the model is not an operator-published map of live beam boundaries.

Map imagery is provided by OpenStreetMap contributors. Country boundaries use public-domain Natural Earth data.

Country spectrum records follow the updated Table 3.5: 31 countries and 34 operator records. Country borders are always visible. Operator cards show service status, paired uplink/downlink frequencies, LTE Band and PLMN. Service colors do not imply that every location can connect at this moment.

Research is selected from the thesis bibliography.
